Simple Tips for Digital Balance

1

Enable Grayscale Screen Mode

Social media apps use hyper-saturated red and neon notification badges engineered by behavioral psychologists to trigger dopamine surges. Turn your screen black & white!

iOS: Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text > Color Filters > On
Android: Settings > Digital Wellbeing > Bedtime Mode > Grayscale
2

Disable All Non-Human Notifications

Allow notifications ONLY from direct messages from real family & friends. Silence algorithmic suggestions, "X posted for the first time in a while", and sales pings.

3

Keep Your Phone Outside the Bedroom

Buy a $10 analog alarm clock. Charging your phone across the room or in another space guarantees you wake up with your own thoughts rather than someone else's feed.
