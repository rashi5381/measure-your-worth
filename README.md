# Don't Measure Your Worth in Likes 🌿💖

An interactive, single-page, mobile-first web application designed for campus digital well-being campaigns, accessed via QR code scans from the MBA poster *"Don't Measure Your Worth in Likes"*.

**Live Site:** [https://amit2303.github.io/dont-measure-your-worth/](https://amit2303.github.io/dont-measure-your-worth/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **⏱️ 60-Second Digital Well-Being Checkup:** 4-question interactive self-reflection quiz with dynamic scoring, empathetic persona analysis, and tailored wellness advice.
- **📓 7-Day Social Media Reset Challenge:** Interactive spiral-notebook checklist with local storage persistence, celebration confetti, and daily mood reflections.
- **💖 Live Digital Pledge Wall:** Incremental campus pledge counter, custom certificate badge generation, and recent pledgers ticker.
- **🎁 Free Digital Toolbox (2x2 Bento Grid):**
  - **💡 Simple Tips:** Grayscale mode simulator, notification curation, and bedroom phone limits.
  - **📊 Screen-Time Life Calculator:** Convert daily screen hours into yearly life equivalents.
  - **💖 5-Min Mindfulness Tools:** Interactive Box Breathing pacer (4-4-4-4s) & 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding.
  - **📖 Trusted Resources:** Curated digital wellness apps and 24/7 mental health emergency helplines.
  - **🖨️ Printable Habit Journal:** Ready-to-print bullet-journal template.

## 🎨 Design System
- **Style:** Neo-Brutalism meets Cozy Sketchbook / Bullet Journal.
- **Palette:** Warm cream paper texture (`#FDFBF7`), Bubblegum Pink, Highlighter Yellow, Mint Green, Lavender, Sky Blue, and ink-black borders (`2.5px solid #1E1E1E`) with hard offset drop shadows (`4px 4px 0px #1E1E1E`).
- **Sound & Accessibility:** Built-in synthesized Web Audio pops, chimes, and fanfare chimes (with mute toggle).
